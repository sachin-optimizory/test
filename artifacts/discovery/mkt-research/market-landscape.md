# Market Landscape — Risk Register with Severity Scoring (Jira) — 2025

## Category Definition

**Primary category**: Project Risk Management tooling for Jira / Atlassian ecosystem
**Adjacent categories**: GRC (Governance, Risk & Compliance), Agile project management, Engineering compliance tooling

---

## Market Size & Dynamics

### Atlassian Ecosystem Scale
- Atlassian serves 300,000+ customers globally (as of 2024 annual report)
- Jira Cloud is the dominant deployment, with ongoing Server → Cloud migration pressure
- Atlassian Marketplace has 5,000+ apps; risk management is a small but growing category
- Forge platform adoption accelerating — Atlassian is actively incentivizing Forge-native apps

### Risk Management Category Trends

**Growing signals:**
- **Regulatory pressure increasing**: DORA (Digital Operational Resilience Act) effective Jan 2025 for EU financial services; SOC 2 Type II becoming standard for SaaS companies; ISO 27001 adoption growing in tech sector
- **"Shift left" risk thinking**: Engineering teams increasingly expected to own risk, not just compliance teams
- **Remote/distributed teams**: Risk visibility harder when teams are distributed — tooling demand increases
- **AI-generated code risk**: New category of risk (AI hallucination, IP risk, security vulnerabilities) creating new risk register use cases
- **Atlassian Cloud migration**: Teams migrating from Server/DC to Cloud are rebuilding their tooling stack — prime moment to adopt new apps

**Declining signals:**
- Standalone spreadsheet-based risk management (Excel/Google Sheets) — teams outgrowing this
- Heavy GRC platforms for non-enterprise teams — too expensive, too complex

### Search Trend Signals (Google Trends / SEO)
- "Risk register Jira" — steady search volume, low competition
- "Risk management Jira plugin" — growing
- "ISO 27001 Jira" — growing, compliance angle
- "Risk matrix template Jira" — moderate volume, content opportunity
- "DORA compliance Jira" — emerging, low competition, high intent

- **Free tier**: Most offer free up to 10 users (Marketplace standard)
- **Per-user pricing**: Dominant model, scales with Jira user count

- **Annual discount**: Standard 10–20% discount for annual commitment

### Reddit Signals (r/jira, r/projectmanagement)
- Frequent complaints about Jira's native lack of risk management
- "We're using a spreadsheet alongside Jira" is the most common workaround mentioned
- Positive sentiment toward apps that "feel native" to Jira
- Negative sentiment toward apps that require separate logins or have complex setup

## Regulatory & Compliance Landscape

| Regulation/Standard | Relevance | Risk Register Requirement |
|--------------------|-----------|---------------------------|
| ISO 27001:2022 | Information security | Mandatory risk register with treatment plans |
| SOC 2 Type II | SaaS/cloud companies | Risk assessment documentation required |
| DORA (EU) | Financial services tech | ICT risk management framework, effective Jan 2025 |
| GDPR | EU data processing | Data risk assessments (DPIAs) |
| NIST CSF 2.0 | US federal/enterprise | Risk identification and response framework |
| PCI DSS v4.0 | Payment processing | Risk assessment requirements |

**Implication**: Compliance-driven buyers are a high-intent, high-LTV segment. They have a non-negotiable need (audit evidence) and budget allocated to compliance tooling.

## Technology Trends Affecting the Category

1. **Forge platform maturity**: Atlassian's Forge platform now supports most app use cases; data residency and security advantages are real selling points for compliance buyers
2. **Atlassian Intelligence (AI)**: Atlassian rolling out AI features — opportunity to differentiate with AI-assisted risk scoring or risk identification
3. **Jira Product Discovery (JPD)**: Growing use of JPD for product planning — risk management integration opportunity
4. **Atlassian Analytics**: Cross-project reporting becoming more native — apps that integrate with Analytics have an advantage
5. **Server end-of-life (Feb 2024)**: Server customers now fully migrated or on DC — Cloud-native apps benefit from this migration wave
