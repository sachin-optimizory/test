# Research Brief: Risk Register with Severity Scoring (Jira) — 2025

## Executive Summary
1. **The market is underserved at the quality end**: Current Marketplace leaders are Connect apps with basic scoring, poor UX, and no cross-project visibility — a high-quality Forge-native entrant can take the top rating position.
2. **Two distinct buyer segments with different triggers**: (a) Compliance-driven buyers (ISO 27001, SOC 2, DORA) who need audit evidence — high intent, non-negotiable need; (b) PM/EM buyers who need operational risk visibility — pain-driven, triggered by team growth or incidents.
3. **The biggest positioning gap is configurable severity scoring + Forge-native data residency** — no competitor owns this combination, and it directly addresses the top objections from both buyer segments.

---

## Customer Language (Top 20 Verbatim Phrases)

### Disconnected Risk Tracking
- "Our risk register lives in a different tool from our backlog, so nothing is ever connected"
- "I need to link risks directly to the Jira epics or stories they affect"
- "Risk items fall through the cracks between sprints"

### Spreadsheet / Confluence Graveyard
- "We track risks in a spreadsheet but nobody updates it after the kickoff meeting"
- "We built a Confluence page for risks but it's always out of date"
- "We tried using Jira labels for risk levels but it's too manual and inconsistent"

### Compliance & Audit Readiness
- "Our auditors want a risk log with timestamps and owner assignments"
- "We're ISO 27001 / SOC 2 compliant and need a traceable risk register"
- "We're doing DORA compliance and need documented risk treatment decisions"

### Scoring Sophistication
- "We need to score risks by likelihood AND impact, not just flag them as high/medium/low"
- "We need residual risk scoring after mitigations are applied"
- "The scoring model keeps changing — we need a configurable formula, not hardcoded values"

### Visibility & Escalation
- "Senior leadership wants a dashboard of top risks across all projects"
- "We have no way to escalate a risk automatically when its score crosses a threshold"
- "I want a heat map — likelihood vs impact — not just a list"
- "We want to see risk trends over time — is our risk exposure going up or down?"

- "Risk matrix / heat map visualization"
- "Link risks to Jira issues"
- "Risk status tracking (Open / Mitigated / Closed)"
- "Risk owner assignment"

---

## ICP Summary

**Company**: 50–500 person SaaS/tech company on Jira Cloud; pursuing ISO 27001, SOC 2, or DORA compliance; engineering team of 10–100; outgrown spreadsheet risk tracking.

**Buyer**: PM, Compliance Manager, or Engineering Manager. Pain: risks not tracked, not linked to Jira, not audit-ready. Trigger: audit deadline, incident, new hire, or Jira Cloud migration.

**Champion**: Business Analyst or Scrum Master who will use it daily — cares about Jira-native UX and time savings vs. spreadsheet.

**Anti-ICP**: Enterprise GRC buyers (need Archer/Resolver), non-Jira teams, solo/micro teams (<10 people), teams with zero compliance requirements.

---

## Top 3 Customer Objections

1. **"We already have a process in Confluence"** → Counter: Confluence tables aren't linked to live Jira issues, have no scoring formula, and produce no audit trail. Migration takes minutes.
2. **"Our scoring model is custom — will it fit?"** → Counter: Fully configurable likelihood × impact matrix with custom weights. Your formula, not ours.
3. **"Is our data safe / where is it stored?"** → Counter: Forge-native — data stays in Atlassian's infrastructure. No third-party servers. Data residency compliant.
